# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Kotlin + Jetpack Compose (native Android).

## Users

Elderly people (lansia), who use the app themselves on their own Android phone. Caregivers, family members and health workers are not confirmed audiences.

## Product Purpose

AplikasiLansia helps elderly users stay mentally active and keep an eye on their health. It does three jobs:

1. **Keeping the brain active:** short daily activities that exercise memory and thinking.
2. **Health tracking:** recording and reviewing their own health information.
3. **Information and education:** health tips and guidance written for older adults.

What success looks like is still open. Candidates include daily activity streaks, how consistently users log their health, and how much users read.

## Positioning

Still open. The confirmed differentiator so far is the combination: brain-activity exercises, personal health tracking and health education in one app built for elderly users rather than general consumers. No competitive claim has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

- Used independently by the elderly person, on an Android phone.
- Built around daily, repeat use: a short activity session and a quick health check-in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Interface language:** Bahasa Indonesia only.
- **Brain activities (confirmed types):**
  - Memory games, such as card matching and recall exercises.
  - Puzzles and logic, such as simple puzzles and number or word games.
  - Daily quiz and trivia.
- **Health tracking:** in scope. Which measurements to track (for example blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, medication or checkup schedules) is still open.
- **Information and education:** in scope. Where the content comes from and who reviews it is still open.
- **Still open:** accounts or sign-in, cloud sync versus on-device storage, reminders and notifications, and emergency or SOS features. None of these has been requested yet.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The repository has no app code, content, data, testimonials or brand assets. Future work must not invent users, endorsements, medical claims or health statistics.

## Product Principles

1. **Built for the elderly person first.** Every flow must work for an older adult using the phone alone, without help.
2. **Short and repeatable.** Activities and check-ins fit into a few minutes and invite users back each day.
3. **Encouraging, never testing.** Brain activities build confidence. Mistakes are not framed as failures or signs of decline.
4. **Trustworthy health content.** Health information is cautious and clear. It never replaces advice from a doctor or other health professional.
5. **Plain Indonesian.** Language is simple, polite and familiar, without jargon or English loanwords where a common Indonesian word exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The primary audience is elderly, so these needs follow from the confirmed users. No formal standard has been chosen yet.

- Large, readable text that respects the system font scale.
- High contrast.
- Generous touch targets.
- Interactions that are forgiving and don't depend on timing.
- No reliance on fine motor gestures.
- Clear feedback for every action.
